# Enterprise Refund Policy

Enterprise customers or with REF-ENT-004 in Germany may request refunds through billing portal.
Approved Enterprise refunds are processed within 45 days.
Customers must provide their invoice number.

# Standard Refund Policy

Standard customers may request refunds within 14 days.
Refund requests must be submitted through the billing portal.

# Premium Cancellation Policy

Premium customers may cancel their subscription within 30 days.
Cancellation requests can be submitted from the account settings page.

# Employee Vacation Policy

Employees in Germany receive 25 vacation per year.
Unused vacation days may be carried over according to company policy.
